Atrial fibrillation evidence:
Atrial fibrillation may present with palpitations, irregular rhythm, chest discomfort, breathlessness, fatigue, or dizziness. A formal 12-lead ECG is commonly used to confirm rhythm abnormality. If symptoms are intermittent, ambulatory rhythm monitoring such as Holter monitoring or an event recorder may be considered.

Clinical relevance:
In a patient with palpitations, elevated heart rate, and an ECG report describing irregular rhythm or possible atrial fibrillation, atrial fibrillation or another supraventricular arrhythmia should be considered as a possible cardiac explanation.

Source:
NICE atrial fibrillation guidance and standard cardiology clinical assessment principles.
