Chest pain evidence:
Chest pain can be caused by cardiac and non-cardiac conditions. Cardiac causes may include angina, acute coronary syndrome, arrhythmia-related symptoms, or structural heart disease. Assessment commonly considers symptoms, ECG findings, vital signs, cardiovascular risk factors, and cardiac biomarkers such as troponin.

Clinical relevance:
In a patient with chest pain that worsens on exertion, clinicians may consider ECG assessment, troponin testing, echocardiography, and further cardiac investigation depending on urgency and clinical judgement.

Source:
NICE chest pain guidance and standard cardiology clinical assessment principles.
