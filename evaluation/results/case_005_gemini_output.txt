### 1. Possible Cardiac Considerations

*   **Heart Failure Syndrome (Moderate confidence):** The constellation of exertional shortness of breath, ankle swelling, and fatigue, particularly in a patient with a history of hypertension and type 2 diabetes, is highly suggestive of heart failure. The ECG finding of left ventricular hypertrophy may reflect underlying cardiac structural changes. However, specific heart failure type (e.g., heart failure with preserved ejection fraction) cannot be determined without further investigation.
*   **Hypertensive Heart Disease with Left Ventricular Hypertrophy (High confidence):** Given the patient's history of hypertension, current elevated blood pressure (148/90 mmHg), and ECG evidence of voltage criteria for left ventricular hypertrophy, chronic pressure overload on the heart is a strong consideration. This can lead to impaired ventricular function and contribute to the reported symptoms.
*   **Coronary Artery Disease / Myocardial Ischaemia (Low confidence):** Hypertension and type 2 diabetes are significant risk factors for coronary artery disease. While no chest pain was reported, exertional shortness of breath can sometimes be an equivalent symptom of myocardial ischaemia. Further evaluation is needed to assess this possibility.

*   **Review of Current Medications:** To assess for potential contributing factors or optimize treatment.
